# Rent Manager - Priority List & Key Focus Areas

## 🎯 Core Daily Operations (Critical - Daily/Weekly)

### 1. **Payment Tracking & Collection** ⭐⭐⭐
**Why it's critical:** Cash flow is the lifeblood of property management
- Track monthly rent payments (due dates, received dates)
- Identify late payments immediately
- Send payment reminders (automated)
- Process partial payments
- Track payment methods (bank transfer, cash, check)
- Generate payment receipts
- Calculate late fees automatically
- Track outstanding balances
- Payment history per tenant
- **Automatic debt calculation** - System calculates what each tenant owes based on all movements
- **Real-time payment dashboard** - See pending payments, paid rents, and economic balance at a glance
- **One-click debt settlement** - Liquidate debts easily in a few clicks
- **Bank reconciliation** - Automate charges and payments, plan SEPA payments, integrate with online payment options
- **Automated invoices and receipts** - Automate invoicing and receipts with customizable templates
- **Pain point:** Missing a late payment = lost revenue

**💡 Insight from [OhMyRent](https://ohmyrent.com/):** They emphasize automatic calculation of tenant debt each month, considering shared expenses and registered income. The system sends emails with updated details of what each tenant owes.

**💡 Insight from [Rentger](https://www.rentger.com/):** They focus on bank reconciliation - automating charges and payments, planning SEPA payments, and integrating with different online payment options. They also automate invoice and receipt generation with customizable templates.

### 2. **Tenant Communication** ⭐⭐⭐
**Why it's critical:** Good communication prevents problems
- Quick access to tenant contact info (phone, email)
- Send bulk messages (maintenance, notices, reminders)
- Track communication history
- Emergency contact management
- Preferred communication method per tenant
- **Automatic email notifications** - Send automatic notices about amounts to pay, eliminating delays or confusion
- **Automatic expense notifications** - Email tenants with expense details automatically
- **Payment reminders** - Automated reminders without manual intervention
- **Transparency** - Clear, automatic, professional communication to improve relationship and avoid misunderstandings
- **Pain point:** Can't reach tenant = delayed rent, unresolved issues

**💡 Insight from [OhMyRent](https://ohmyrent.com/):** They automate all tenant notifications - payments, expenses, and reminders. Tenants receive automatic emails with payment details, ensuring transparency and eliminating the need for manual follow-ups.

### 3. **Maintenance Requests / Incident Management** ⭐⭐⭐
**Why it's critical:** Unresolved maintenance = unhappy tenants = turnover
- Log maintenance requests immediately
- Prioritize (urgent, normal, cosmetic)
- Assign to contractors/vendors
- Track status (reported → in progress → completed)
- Photo documentation (before/after)
- Cost tracking per request
- Tenant satisfaction after completion
- Recurring issues tracking
- **Online incident management** - Manage and document incidents with tenants, landlords, and suppliers online
- **Incident notifications** - Automatic notifications for incidents
- **Pain point:** Slow response = tenant complaints, legal issues

**💡 Insight from [Rentger](https://www.rentger.com/):** They emphasize incident management - manage and document incidents with tenants, landlords, and suppliers online. They track over 33,000 resolved incidents, showing this is a critical feature.

### 4. **Lease Management** ⭐⭐⭐
**Why it's critical:** Legal compliance and revenue protection
- Track lease start/end dates
- Automatic renewal reminders (30/60/90 days before)
- Lease terms (rent amount, deposit, pet fees, utilities)
- Document storage (signed lease, addendums)
- Track lease violations
- Early termination handling
- **Digital signature included** - Generate contracts automatically and send them to sign digitally with full legal validity
- **Automatic rent updates** - Update rents automatically via IPC (inflation index), calculated updates, or specific amount
- **Contract expiration notices** - Automatic notifications for contract expirations
- **Rent increase notices** - Automatic notifications for rent increases
- **Pain point:** Expired lease = month-to-month uncertainty, lost revenue

**💡 Insight from [Rentger](https://www.rentger.com/):** They include digital signature functionality - automatically generate contracts and send them for digital signing with full legal validity. They also automate rent updates based on IPC (inflation index) or calculated amounts, and send automatic notices for contract expirations and rent increases.

## 🗄️ Database Schema Design for Rent Management

### New Tables to Add

Based on the priorities above and insights from [OhMyRent](https://ohmyrent.com/), here are the new tables needed and how they relate to your existing schema:

#### 1. **RENT_CONTRACTS** (Leases)
**Purpose:** Core table for rent management - stores lease agreements

**Relationships:**
- `listing_id` → `listings.listing_id` (Required - links to rent listing)
- `listing_contact_id` → `listing_contacts.listing_contact_id` (Required - tenant relationship)
- `landlord_contact_id` → `contacts.contact_id` (Optional - if landlord is a contact)
- `property_id` → `properties.property_id` (Derived from listing, but direct link for performance)

**Key Fields:**
- `contract_id` (PK)
- `account_id` (FK → accounts) - Multi-tenant
- `lease_start_date`, `lease_end_date`
- `monthly_rent` (decimal)
- `deposit_amount` (decimal)
- `utilities_included` (boolean)
- `lease_terms` (JSONB - flexible terms)
- `status` ('active', 'expired', 'terminated', 'renewed')
- `renewal_reminder_sent` (boolean)
- `auto_renew` (boolean)
- `rent_update_method` ('none', 'ipc', 'calculated', 'fixed_amount') - How to update rent
- `last_rent_update_date` (timestamp)
- `next_rent_update_date` (timestamp)
- `digital_signature_status` ('pending', 'signed', 'expired') - Digital signature tracking
- `contract_document_id` → `documents.doc_id` (FK - signed contract document)

**Why separate from DEALS?**
- Rent contracts have different lifecycle than sales deals
- Need to track monthly payments over time
- Different terms and conditions
- Can have multiple contracts per property (room rentals)

---

#### 2. **RENT_PAYMENTS**
**Purpose:** Track all rent payments and calculate automatic debt

**Relationships:**
- `rent_contract_id` → `rent_contracts.contract_id` (Required)
- `listing_contact_id` → `listing_contacts.listing_contact_id` (Required - which tenant paid)
- `property_id` → `properties.property_id` (For reporting/performance)

**Key Fields:**
- `payment_id` (PK)
- `account_id` (FK → accounts) - Multi-tenant
- `payment_type` ('rent', 'deposit', 'expense', 'fee', 'late_fee')
- `amount` (decimal)
- `due_date` (timestamp)
- `paid_date` (timestamp, nullable)
- `payment_method` ('bank_transfer', 'cash', 'check', 'online', 'sepa')
- `status` ('pending', 'paid', 'late', 'partial', 'overdue')
- `reference_number` (varchar - bank reference, check number)
- `sepa_mandate_id` (varchar - SEPA direct debit mandate ID)
- `bank_reconciliation_status` ('pending', 'reconciled', 'discrepancy')
- `bank_reconciliation_date` (timestamp, nullable)
- `receipt_generated` (boolean)
- `receipt_document_id` → `documents.doc_id` (FK - generated receipt)
- `invoice_generated` (boolean)
- `invoice_document_id` → `documents.doc_id` (FK - generated invoice)
- `notes` (text)

**Automatic Debt Calculation:**
- System calculates: `monthly_rent + shared_expenses - payments_received = current_debt`
- This is the core feature from OhMyRent

---

#### 3. **RENT_EXPENSES** (Shared Expenses)
**Purpose:** Track shared expenses and automatically distribute them

**Relationships:**
- `rent_contract_id` → `rent_contracts.contract_id` (Required - which contract)
- `property_id` → `properties.property_id` (Required - which property)
- `account_id` → `accounts.account_id` (Multi-tenant)

**Key Fields:**
- `expense_id` (PK)
- `expense_type` ('utilities', 'community_fees', 'insurance', 'maintenance', 'other')
- `description` (text)
- `total_amount` (decimal)
- `expense_date` (timestamp)
- `distribution_method` ('equal', 'percentage', 'fixed_amount', 'by_usage')
- `distribution_config` (JSONB - how to split: `{ tenant1_id: 0.5, tenant2_id: 0.5 }` or `{ tenant1_id: 30, tenant2_id: 70 }`)
- `distributed_amounts` (JSONB - calculated amounts per tenant)
- `status` ('pending', 'distributed', 'paid')
- `invoice_url` (varchar - link to invoice document)
- `document_id` → `documents.doc_id` (Optional - link to uploaded invoice)

**Automatic Distribution:**
- System calculates each tenant's share based on `distribution_method`
- Creates entries in `rent_expense_distributions` table
- Sends automatic email notifications to tenants

---

#### 4. **RENT_EXPENSE_DISTRIBUTIONS**
**Purpose:** Junction table - tracks how each expense is distributed to each tenant

**Relationships:**
- `expense_id` → `rent_expenses.expense_id` (Required)
- `listing_contact_id` → `listing_contacts.listing_contact_id` (Required - which tenant)
- `rent_contract_id` → `rent_contracts.contract_id` (Required)

**Key Fields:**
- `distribution_id` (PK)
- `tenant_share_amount` (decimal - calculated amount for this tenant)
- `percentage` (decimal - if percentage-based)
- `fixed_amount` (decimal - if fixed amount)
- `notification_sent` (boolean)
- `notification_sent_at` (timestamp)

**Why separate table?**
- One expense can be split among multiple tenants
- Track individual tenant shares
- Track notification status per tenant

---

#### 5. **RENT_MAINTENANCE_REQUESTS** (Incident Management)
**Purpose:** Track maintenance issues and incidents for rental properties

**Relationships:**
- `rent_contract_id` → `rent_contracts.contract_id` (Required)
- `property_id` → `properties.property_id` (Required)
- `listing_contact_id` → `listing_contacts.listing_contact_id` (Required - tenant who reported)
- `task_id` → `tasks.task_id` (Optional - link to existing task system)
- `supplier_contact_id` → `contacts.contact_id` (Optional - contractor/supplier)

**Key Fields:**
- `maintenance_id` (PK)
- `account_id` → `accounts.account_id` (Multi-tenant)
- `incident_type` ('maintenance', 'complaint', 'violation', 'emergency', 'other')
- `issue_description` (text)
- `priority` ('urgent', 'high', 'normal', 'low')
- `status` ('reported', 'acknowledged', 'in_progress', 'completed', 'cancelled', 'resolved')
- `reported_date` (timestamp)
- `acknowledged_date` (timestamp, nullable)
- `completed_date` (timestamp, nullable)
- `resolved_date` (timestamp, nullable)
- `cost` (decimal, nullable)
- `contractor_name` (varchar)
- `supplier_contact_id` → `contacts.contact_id` (FK - contractor/supplier)
- `before_photos` (JSONB - array of image URLs)
- `after_photos` (JSONB - array of image URLs)
- `tenant_satisfaction` (smallint - 1-5 rating)
- `documentation` (JSONB - array of document IDs)
- `resolution_notes` (text)

**Integration:**
- Can create `tasks` automatically from maintenance requests
- Can link to `appointments` for contractor visits

**Key Fields:**
- `deposit_id` (PK)
- `account_id` → `accounts.account_id` (Multi-tenant)

---

#### 7. **RENT_RENEWALS**
**Purpose:** Track lease renewals and rent increases

**Relationships:**
- `rent_contract_id` → `rent_contracts.contract_id` (Required - original contract)
- `new_contract_id` → `rent_contracts.contract_id` (Nullable - new contract if renewed)

**Key Fields:**
- `renewal_id` (PK)
- `account_id` → `accounts.account_id` (Multi-tenant)
- `renewal_type` ('renewal', 'rent_increase', 'extension', 'ipc_update')
- `update_method` ('ipc', 'calculated', 'fixed_amount', 'market_rate')
- `ipc_percentage` (decimal, nullable - if IPC-based)
- `old_monthly_rent` (decimal)
- `new_monthly_rent` (decimal)
- `renewal_date` (timestamp)
- `status` ('pending', 'accepted', 'rejected', 'expired')
- `notice_sent_date` (timestamp)
- `tenant_response_date` (timestamp, nullable)
- `notice_document_id` → `documents.doc_id` (FK - rent increase notice)

### Key Relationships Explained

#### RENT_CONTRACTS ↔ LISTINGS
- **Relationship:** 1:1 (One rent contract per listing, but listing can have multiple contracts over time)
- **Link:** `rent_contracts.listing_id` → `listings.listing_id`
- **Filter:** Only for `listings.listingType = 'Rent'`
- **Purpose:** Contract is created when tenant signs lease for a rent listing

#### RENT_CONTRACTS ↔ LISTING_CONTACTS
- **Relationship:** 1:N (One contract can have multiple tenants - room rentals)
- **Link:** `rent_contracts.listing_contact_id` → `listing_contacts.listing_contact_id`
- **Purpose:** Links contract to tenant relationship
- **Note:** For room rentals, you might have multiple `listing_contacts` with `contactType='tenant'` per contract

#### RENT_PAYMENTS ↔ RENT_CONTRACTS
- **Relationship:** 1:N (One contract has many payments)
- **Link:** `rent_payments.rent_contract_id` → `rent_contracts.contract_id`
- **Purpose:** Track all payments for a lease

#### RENT_EXPENSES ↔ RENT_CONTRACTS
- **Relationship:** 1:N (One contract has many expenses)
- **Link:** `rent_expenses.rent_contract_id` → `rent_contracts.contract_id`
- **Purpose:** Track shared expenses that need to be distributed

#### RENT_EXPENSE_DISTRIBUTIONS ↔ RENT_EXPENSES
- **Relationship:** 1:N (One expense distributed to multiple tenants)
- **Link:** `rent_expense_distributions.expense_id` → `rent_expenses.expense_id`
- **Purpose:** Track individual tenant shares of each expense

#### RENT_MAINTENANCE_REQUESTS ↔ RENT_CONTRACTS
- **Relationship:** 1:N (One contract has many maintenance requests)
- **Link:** `rent_maintenance_requests.rent_contract_id` → `rent_contracts.contract_id`
- **Purpose:** Track maintenance issues for rental properties

### Integration with Existing Tables

**✅ Reuse Existing:**
- **CONTACTS** - Tenants and landlords are contacts (use `contactType='tenant'` or `'landlord'` in `listing_contacts`)
- **PROPERTIES** - Rent contracts link to properties via listings
- **LISTINGS** - Rent listings already exist (`listingType='Rent'`)
- **LISTING_CONTACTS** - Tenant relationships already supported
- **TASKS** - Can create tasks from maintenance requests
- **APPOINTMENTS** - Use for inspections, contractor visits
- **DOCUMENTS** - Store leases, invoices, receipts
- **NOTIFICATIONS** - Use for payment reminders, expense notifications

**🆕 New Tables Needed:**
1. `rent_contracts` - Core lease management (with digital signature support, IPC rent updates)
2. `rent_payments` - Payment tracking with automatic debt calculation (SEPA, bank reconciliation)
3. `rent_expenses` - Shared expense tracking
4. `rent_expense_distributions` - Expense splitting per tenant
5. `rent_maintenance_requests` - Maintenance/incident tracking (online management)
6. `rent_deposits` - Deposit management
7. `rent_renewals` - Lease renewal tracking (IPC-based updates)
8. `rent_bank_reconciliation` - Bank reconciliation records (optional, or add to rent_payments)
9. `rent_digital_signatures` - Digital signature tracking (optional, or add to rent_contracts)
10. `rent_invoices` - Automated invoice generation (optional, or add to rent_payments)

### Design Decisions

1. **Separate from DEALS table?**
   - **Yes** - Rent contracts have different lifecycle than sales deals
   - Rent = ongoing monthly relationship, Sales = one-time transaction
   - Different fields needed (monthly rent vs final price)

2. **Use LISTING_CONTACTS for tenants?**
   - **Yes** - Already supports `contactType='tenant'` or `'landlord'`
   - Reuses existing relationship structure
   - Can track tenant history across multiple properties

3. **Link to PROPERTIES directly?**
   - **Yes** - For performance (avoid JOIN through listings)
   - Also link via `listing_id` for data integrity
   - Both links provide flexibility

4. **Automatic Debt Calculation?**
   - Calculated field: `monthly_rent + expense_shares - payments_received`
   - Stored in `rent_contracts.current_debt` (updated on payment/expense changes)
   - Or calculated on-the-fly for real-time accuracy

5. **Bank Reconciliation?**
   - **Yes** - Add fields to `rent_payments` for SEPA, bank reconciliation
   - Track SEPA mandate IDs, reconciliation status
   - Link to bank statements for automatic matching

6. **Digital Signature?**
   - **Yes** - Add to `rent_contracts` or create separate `rent_digital_signatures` table
   - Track signature status, signed documents
   - Store signature provider (e.g., DocuSign, SignNow) and signature IDs

7. **IPC-Based Rent Updates?**
   - **Yes** - Add `rent_update_method` to `rent_contracts`
   - Store IPC percentage, last update date, next update date
   - Automatic calculation based on Spanish inflation index

8. **Incident vs Maintenance?**
   - **Unified approach** - Use `rent_maintenance_requests` for both
   - Add `incident_type` field to distinguish maintenance, complaints, violations
   - Can link to suppliers/contractors via `contacts` table

### Example Data Flow

**Creating a Rent Contract:**
1. Property exists in `properties`
2. Create rent `listing` (listingType='Rent')
3. Create `listing_contact` (contactType='tenant') linking tenant to listing
4. Create `rent_contract` linking to listing and listing_contact
5. Create initial `rent_deposit` record
6. Create first `rent_payment` (monthly rent, due date = lease start)

**Monthly Payment:**
1. System creates `rent_payment` record (due_date = 1st of month)
2. Tenant pays → update `rent_payment.paid_date`
3. System recalculates `rent_contract.current_debt`
4. Send automatic receipt email

**Shared Expense:**
1. Create `rent_expense` (e.g., utilities bill = €100)
2. System calculates distribution based on `distribution_method`
3. Creates `rent_expense_distributions` records (e.g., 2 tenants = €50 each)
4. Updates `rent_contract.current_debt` for each tenant
5. Sends automatic email to each tenant with their share
6. Link invoice to `documents` table

**Maintenance Request:**
1. Tenant reports issue → create `rent_maintenance_request`
2. Optionally create `task` for follow-up
3. Schedule `appointment` for contractor visit
4. Update status as work progresses
5. Link photos to `documents` table
6. Record cost in `rent_expense` if landlord pays

This design leverages your existing schema while adding the specialized tables needed for comprehensive rent management.
